# Digital Research Methods

**Dr Eva Ferron** · Digital politics · Computational social science · Research methods education

An evolving collection of **practical, reproducible examples for humanities and social science researchers**, connecting questions to data preparation, analysis and interpretable outputs.

**Status:** Two openly accessible, introductory educational demonstrations. Both are new, AI-assisted drafts for further review and teaching development, **not previously delivered CDCS/EFI courses or peer-reviewed outputs**.

## Published tutorials

### [1. From Synthetic Platform Data to Social Network Analysis](tutorials/social-media-to-network/README.md)

A guided example using **Python, pandas and matplotlib**. Clean a fictional social-media-style export, summarise posting activity and engagement, and produce a weighted, directed edge list for Gephi. **No real social media data or live API access**.

### [2. From API-style JSON to Validated Research Data](tutorials/api-json-to-sql/README.md)

A supplementary exercise developed after reviewing the original **2023 Data with Python** course materials in Google Colab. Use a wholly fictional JSON fixture, validate missing and duplicate records, clean with **pandas**, query the result with **SQLite**, produce a data-quality report and run **automated tests**. The exercise runs offline and does not make live API requests.

## Research and methods background

My research combines API-derived social media data, social network analysis and corpus-assisted text analysis, including **word frequencies, keyness, collocations and concordances (KWIC)**. I am particularly interested in transparent computational workflows and accessible, critical research-methods instruction.

## Research integrity, reuse and attribution

- All accounts and records in these tutorials are **synthetic**, not doctoral research data.
- The original 2023 Colab notebooks remain in a **separate private archive**. These new tutorials are not backfilled or retrospectively claimed course submissions.
- AI assistance contributed to drafting the teaching examples; independent review, learner testing and appropriate licensing remain important before formal course deployment.
- [CITATION.cff](CITATION.cff) provides provisional metadata for attributing the repository. A DOI or citation metadata is **not evidence of peer review**.
- Reuse and redistribution permissions for original material will be clarified in a future licence. Please contact the maintainer for permission in the meantime.
